📖 Grade 9 Geography Notes

Welcome to Grade 9 Geography Notes. Choose any unit below to study the lesson in detail.

The notes are organized according to the Grade 9 Geography curriculum.

🌍 Unit 1 — Geography and Its Concepts

Study the basic concepts of geography, geographical location, place, environment, interaction and spatial relationships.

🗺️ Unit 2 — Map Reading and Interpretation

Learn about maps, map symbols, direction, scale, coordinates and interpretation of geographic information.

🌎 Unit 3 — Physical Geography of Ethiopia

Study the physical environment of Ethiopia, including relief, drainage, climate, soils and natural vegetation.

🌦️ Unit 4 — Climate of Ethiopia

Learn about the climate of Ethiopia, temperature, rainfall, climatic controls, seasons and climatic regions.

🌱 Unit 5 — Natural Resources and Environment

Study Ethiopia's natural resources, environmental management, conservation and the relationship between people and nature.

👥 Unit 6 — Population and Settlement

Learn about population distribution, population growth, migration, settlements and related geographic issues.

🌿 Unit 7 — Contemporary Geographic Issues and Public Concerns in Ethiopia

Study contemporary geographic and environmental issues including land degradation, deforestation, soil erosion, biodiversity, climate change, pollution and environmental conservation.

🧭 Unit 8 — Geographical Enquiry, Skills and Techniques

Learn geographical enquiry, map skills, scale, distance and area measurement, grid references, map sketching, fieldwork and interpretation of maps, graphs and tables.
