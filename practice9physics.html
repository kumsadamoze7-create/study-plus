⚛️ Unit 1 — Physics and Its Applications

Practice the meaning of physics, branches of physics, scientific investigation and applications of physics in everyday life and technology.

Practice physical quantities, SI units, measurement, measuring instruments, accuracy, precision and measurement errors.

Practice distance, displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration and graphical representation of motion.

Practice force, balanced and unbalanced forces, Newton's laws of motion, mass, weight and applications of force.

Practice levers, pulleys, inclined planes, mechanical advantage, velocity ratio and efficiency of simple machines.

🔊 Unit 6 — Mechanical Oscillation and Sound Wave

Practice oscillatory motion, vibration, amplitude, frequency, period, sound waves, wavelength, frequency and wave speed.

🌡️ Unit 7 — Temperature and Thermometer

Practice temperature, thermometers, temperature scales, Celsius, Fahrenheit and measurement of temperature.
