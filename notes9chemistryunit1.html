🧪 Unit 1: Chemistry and Its Importance

Chemistry is one of the important branches of natural science. It helps us understand the substances around us, how they are made, what properties they have, and how they change.

Chemistry is not limited to the laboratory. It is connected with medicine, agriculture, food production, construction, industry, environmental protection and almost every part of modern life.

1.1 Definition and Scope of Chemistry

Definition of Chemistry

Chemistry is the branch of natural science that studies matter, its composition, structure, properties and the changes it undergoes.

Chemistry tries to answer questions such as: What is a substance made of? Why does it have a particular property? What happens when it reacts with another substance? How can useful materials be produced?

Why is Chemistry Important?

Everything around us is made of matter. Water, air, food, medicines, metals, plastics, fuels and living organisms all involve chemical substances and chemical processes.

📦 Matter

Matter is anything that has mass and occupies space.

Examples of Matter

  • Water
  • Air
  • Wood
  • Iron
  • Food
  • Plastic
  • Human body

Matter can exist in different states such as solid, liquid and gas. Chemistry studies the particles that make up matter and the changes between different states and substances.

🧱 Substance

A substance is a form of matter that has a definite composition and characteristic properties.

Examples

  • Water (H₂O)
  • Oxygen (O₂)
  • Iron (Fe)
  • Sodium chloride (NaCl)

Each substance has properties that can help us identify it. For example, water has particular melting and boiling points, while iron has characteristic physical and chemical properties.

🔍 Property

A property is a characteristic of a substance that can be observed or measured.

Physical Properties

Physical properties can be observed or measured without changing the substance into another substance.

  • Color
  • Density
  • Melting point
  • Boiling point
  • Solubility
  • Electrical conductivity

Chemical Properties

Chemical properties describe how a substance behaves during chemical reactions.

  • Flammability
  • Reactivity with oxygen
  • Reactivity with acids
  • Ability to undergo corrosion

🧩 Composition

Composition refers to the types and relative amounts of substances or particles that make up matter.

For example, water is composed of hydrogen and oxygen. Its chemical formula H₂O shows that water contains hydrogen and oxygen in a definite ratio.

Remember:
Composition tells us what a substance is made of and, in many cases, the relative amount of each component.

🌳 Scope / Branches of Chemistry

1. Organic Chemistry

Organic chemistry studies carbon-containing compounds, especially those associated with living systems and fuels, plastics, medicines and many other materials.

2. Inorganic Chemistry

Inorganic chemistry studies substances such as metals, minerals, salts, acids, bases and many compounds that are not classified as organic compounds.

3. Physical Chemistry

Physical chemistry studies the relationship between matter and energy and explains why chemical processes occur.

4. Analytical Chemistry

Analytical chemistry is concerned with identifying substances and determining how much of a substance is present in a sample.

5. Biochemistry

Biochemistry studies chemical substances and chemical reactions that occur in living organisms.

🔗 Chemistry and Other Natural Sciences

Chemistry is strongly connected with other natural sciences. Many scientific problems cannot be completely understood by studying only one branch of science.

🧬 Chemistry + Biology

Biology studies living organisms, while chemistry explains many of the chemical processes that occur inside them. This relationship is called Biochemistry.

Examples include digestion, respiration, enzymes, proteins, DNA and metabolism.

⚡ Chemistry + Physics

Physics studies matter, energy, forces and motion. Chemistry also studies energy changes and the behavior of matter. Physical chemistry connects these two sciences.

For example, energy changes during chemical reactions can be studied using ideas from physics.

🌍 Chemistry + Earth Science

Chemistry helps scientists understand rocks, minerals, soil, water and the atmosphere. The study of the chemical composition of Earth materials is closely related to Geochemistry.

🌱 Chemistry + Agriculture

Agriculture depends on soil nutrients, fertilizers, pesticides, water quality and plant growth. Chemistry helps determine the chemical composition of soil and fertilizers and how nutrients are used by plants.

🌎 Chemistry + Environmental Science

Chemistry helps us understand air pollution, water pollution, waste treatment, climate-related chemical processes and the movement of chemicals through the environment.

🏭 1.3 Role of Chemistry in Production and Society

Chemistry plays an important role in producing materials and products that people use every day. It also helps improve health, agriculture, food, construction and industry.

🌱 Chemistry in Agriculture

Plants need nutrients such as nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium for healthy growth. Chemistry helps scientists understand these nutrients and produce fertilizers that supply them to crops.

Examples

  • Fertilizers
  • Herbicides
  • Pesticides
  • Soil testing
  • Water-quality testing

Chemistry can also help reduce agricultural losses by improving methods of controlling pests and preserving agricultural products.

🍞 Chemistry in Food Production

Food contains many chemical substances such as carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals and water. Chemistry helps us understand what happens to these substances during cooking, processing and storage.

Applications

  • Food preservation
  • Food processing
  • Quality control
  • Packaging
  • Detection of food contamination
  • Understanding nutritional composition

💊 Chemistry in Medicine

Chemistry is extremely important in medicine. Medicines contain active chemical substances that interact with biological systems. Chemists help discover, prepare, analyze and improve medicines.

Chemistry is also used to determine the composition, purity, stability and appropriate formulation of pharmaceutical products.

💊 Analgesics

Analgesics are medicines used mainly to relieve or reduce pain.

Pain can have many causes. Analgesic medicines work through different biological mechanisms to reduce the sensation of pain. Some also reduce fever or inflammation depending on the medicine.

Example Type Common use
Paracetamol Analgesic / Antipyretic Relief of mild to moderate pain and reduction of fever
Ibuprofen NSAID analgesic Pain relief and reduction of inflammation
Medicines should be used according to appropriate instructions. The correct dose depends on the medicine, age, health condition and other factors.

🦠 Antibiotics

Antibiotics are medicines used to treat certain bacterial infections by killing bacteria or preventing their growth.

Antibiotics are important products of medicinal chemistry. Scientists study the chemical structure and biological activity of these substances to develop medicines that can act against bacteria.

Example

Drug Type Use
Amoxicillin Antibiotic Used to treat certain bacterial infections when prescribed
Important: Antibiotics do not treat viral infections such as the common cold or influenza. They should be taken only when appropriate and according to professional medical advice.

🧴 Disinfectants

Disinfectants are chemical substances used on non-living surfaces to destroy or reduce harmful microorganisms.

Disinfectants are different from medicines. They are generally used on surfaces and objects, not inside the human body.

Examples

  • Alcohol-based disinfectants
  • Sodium hypochlorite solutions
  • Hydrogen peroxide-based disinfectants

Uses

  • Cleaning laboratory surfaces
  • Disinfecting equipment
  • Reducing microorganisms on suitable surfaces
  • Maintaining hygiene in health facilities and homes
Do not confuse:

Antibiotic → used against certain bacteria in the body.
Analgesic → used mainly to relieve pain.
Disinfectant → used to reduce or destroy microorganisms on non-living surfaces.

🏗️ Chemistry in Building Construction

Chemistry helps produce and improve construction materials. The properties of materials determine their strength, durability, resistance to heat and water, and suitability for different uses.

Examples

  • Cement — important binding material in construction.
  • Glass — produced from carefully processed mineral raw materials.
  • Paint — protects and decorates surfaces.
  • Ceramics — used for tiles, bricks and other applications.
  • Steel — widely used because of its useful mechanical properties.
  • Plastics — used in pipes, insulation and many other products.

🏭 1.4 Chemical Industries in Ethiopia

Industry

Industry is an economic activity in which raw materials are processed to produce useful goods.

Chemical Industry

A chemical industry uses chemical processes to transform raw materials into useful chemical products on a large scale.

Chemical industries can contribute to agriculture, health, construction, food production, manufacturing and other sectors.

📦 Three General Classes of Chemical Products

Chemical products can be discussed according to their position in the production process as:

1️⃣ Basic Products

Basic products are fundamental chemicals that can be used as starting materials or inputs for producing other chemicals.

Examples

  • Sulfuric acid (H₂SO₄)
  • Hydrochloric acid (HCl)
  • Sodium hydroxide (NaOH)
  • Ammonia (NH₃)

Uses

  • Manufacturing fertilizers
  • Manufacturing other chemicals
  • Industrial processing
  • Water and material treatment in suitable processes

2️⃣ Intermediate Products

Intermediate products are substances produced during a manufacturing process that are then used to make other products.

Examples

  • Ethylene
  • Propylene
  • Methanol
  • Other chemical intermediates used to produce polymers and resins

Uses

  • Production of plastics
  • Production of synthetic materials
  • Production of resins
  • Production of paints and coatings

3️⃣ Finished Products

Finished products are products that have completed the required manufacturing process and are ready for their intended use or sale.

Examples

  • Soap
  • Detergent
  • Paint
  • Fertilizers
  • Medicines
  • Cosmetics
  • Adhesives

Uses

Finished chemical products are used directly by consumers, farmers, health workers, builders and industries.

🔄 Relationship Between the Three Product Classes

Basic → Intermediate → Finished Product

A chemical production process can involve several stages. A basic chemical can serve as an input for producing an intermediate substance. The intermediate can then be processed further to make a finished product.

⭐ Unit 1 Summary

  • Chemistry is the study of matter, its composition, structure, properties and changes.
  • Matter has mass and occupies space.
  • A substance has definite composition and characteristic properties.
  • Properties can be physical or chemical.
  • Chemistry has several branches including organic, inorganic, physical, analytical and biochemistry.
  • Chemistry is closely related to biology, physics, agriculture, geology and environmental science.
  • Chemistry plays an important role in agriculture, food production, medicine and construction.
  • Analgesics are mainly used to relieve pain.
  • Antibiotics are used to treat certain bacterial infections.
  • Disinfectants are used mainly on non-living surfaces to reduce microorganisms.
  • Chemical products can be classified as basic, intermediate and finished products according to their stage in production.
