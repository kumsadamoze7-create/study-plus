UNIT 1

Physics and Human Society

Grade 9 Physics • New Ethiopian Curriculum

🎯 Learning Outcomes

At the end of this unit, students should be able to:

  • Define physics in different ways.
  • Describe the different branches of physics.
  • Explain the relationship between physics and other fields of study.
  • Discuss the contributions of prominent scientists to physics.
  • Explain applications of physics in everyday life and technology.
  • Describe how physics knowledge has developed through history.

1.1 Definition and Nature of Physics

Physics is a branch of natural science that studies matter, energy, motion, forces, space and time, and the interactions between them.

What is Physics?

Physics helps us understand how things work in nature. It explains many phenomena that we observe in our daily lives.

💡 Examples of Physics in Everyday Life

  • Movement of cars and bicycles
  • Electricity and electrical devices
  • Mobile phones and communication
  • Light and vision
  • Sound and music
  • Machines and engines

1.2 Branches of Physics

Physics has different branches that study different aspects of the physical world.

⚙️ Mechanics

Studies motion, forces and the behavior of objects.

🔥 Thermodynamics

Studies heat, temperature and energy transfer.

⚡ Electromagnetism

Studies electricity, magnetism and their relationship.

🔭 Optics

Studies light and its behavior.

🔊 Acoustics

Studies sound and its properties.

⚛️ Nuclear Physics

Studies atomic nuclei and nuclear processes.

🌌 Astrophysics

Applies physics to stars, planets and other objects in space.

1.3 Physics and Other Fields of Study

Physics is closely related to many other fields of science and technology.

  • Physics + Biology: helps explain movement, vision and the human body.
  • Physics + Chemistry: helps explain atoms, molecules and energy.
  • Physics + Engineering: helps in designing machines, buildings and technologies.
  • Physics + Medicine: helps in medical imaging and radiation technologies.
  • Physics + Astronomy: helps us understand stars, planets and the universe.

1.4 Physics and Technology

Physics plays an important role in the development of modern technology.

Examples

  • 📱 Mobile phones
  • 💻 Computers
  • 🚗 Vehicles
  • ✈️ Aircraft
  • 🏥 Medical equipment
  • ⚡ Electrical power systems
  • 📡 Communication systems

1.5 Historical Development of Physics

Physics has developed over a long period of human history. Scientists from different periods contributed to our understanding of nature.

🔬 Galileo Galilei

Made important contributions to the study of motion and observation.

🍎 Isaac Newton

Developed important laws of motion and universal gravitation.

⚡ Michael Faraday

Made major contributions to electricity and electromagnetism.

🧠 Albert Einstein

Developed the theory of relativity and made major contributions to modern physics.
