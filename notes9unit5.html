5.1 Revision on Right-Angled Triangles

A right-angled triangle is a triangle that contains one 90° angle.

Parts of a Right Triangle

Hypotenuse: The side opposite the 90° angle. It is always the longest side.

Opposite: The side opposite the angle we are considering.

Adjacent: The side next to the angle we are considering, but not the hypotenuse.

⭐ Important:

Hypotenuse → opposite 90° angle
Opposite → opposite the chosen angle
Adjacent → beside the chosen angle

5.1.1 Pythagoras Theorem

For a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse is equal to the sum of the squares of the other two sides.

c² = a² + b²

where c is the hypotenuse.

Example 1

A right triangle has sides 3 cm and 4 cm. Find the hypotenuse.

c² = 3² + 4²
c² = 9 + 16
c² = 25
c = 5 cm

Example 2

The hypotenuse is 13 cm and one side is 5 cm. Find the other side.

13² = 5² + b²
169 = 25 + b²
b² = 144
b = 12 cm
⚡ Entrance Trick:

Remember these common Pythagorean triples:

3 – 4 – 5
5 – 12 – 13
6 – 8 – 10
8 – 15 – 17

5.2 Trigonometric Ratios

Trigonometric ratios relate the angles of a right triangle to the lengths of its sides.

Three Main Ratios

sin θ = Opposite / Hypotenuse

cos θ = Adjacent / Hypotenuse

tan θ = Opposite / Adjacent
🔥 SOH – CAH – TOA

SOH
Sin = Opposite / Hypotenuse

CAH
Cos = Adjacent / Hypotenuse

TOA
Tan = Opposite / Adjacent

Example: Finding Trigonometric Ratios

Suppose a right triangle has:

  • Opposite = 3
  • Adjacent = 4
  • Hypotenuse = 5
sin θ = 3/5

cos θ = 4/5

tan θ = 3/4
⚡ First identify the three sides. Then choose SOH, CAH or TOA.

5.2.1 Trigonometric Values of Basic Angles

The most important basic angles are:

0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°

Angle sin cos tan
0° 0 1 0
30° 1/2 √3/2 1/√3
45° √2/2 √2/2 1
60° √3/2 1/2 √3
90° 1 0 Undefined

🧠 Easy Memory Trick

Sin 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°

√0/2, √1/2, √2/2, √3/2, √4/2

Therefore:

sin 0° = 0
sin 30° = 1/2
sin 45° = √2/2
sin 60° = √3/2
sin 90° = 1

Cosine

For cosine, read the same values in reverse order.

cos 0° = 1
cos 30° = √3/2
cos 45° = √2/2
cos 60° = 1/2
cos 90° = 0
🔥 Shortcut:

Sin → √0, √1, √2, √3, √4

Cos → √4, √3, √2, √1, √0

All divided by 2.

5.2.2 Tangent Values

Tangent can be calculated using:

tan θ = sin θ / cos θ
tan 30°

= (1/2) / (√3/2)
= 1/√3

tan 45° = 1

tan 60° = √3
⚠️ Important:

tan 90° is undefined because:
tan 90° = sin 90° / cos 90°
= 1 / 0

Division by zero is undefined.

5.2.3 Trigonometric Ratios from 0° to 90°

For angles between 0° and 90°, the three main ratios are positive.

Angle sin θ cos θ tan θ
0° 0 1 0
30° 1/2 √3/2 1/√3
45° √2/2 √2/2 1
60° √3/2 1/2 √3
90° 1 0 Undefined

🎯 Entrance Exam Short Tricks

1. Hypotenuse is always opposite 90°.

2. SOH → sin

3. CAH → cos

4. TOA → tan

5. tan θ = sin θ / cos θ

6. sin 30° = 1/2

7. cos 60° = 1/2

8. sin 90° = 1

9. cos 90° = 0

10. tan 45° = 1

⚠️ Common Mistakes

❌ Confusing opposite and adjacent.

❌ Using the hypotenuse as the adjacent side.

❌ Forgetting that the hypotenuse is opposite 90°.

❌ Mixing sin and cos values.

❌ Saying tan 90° = 0.

❌ Forgetting that tan 90° is undefined.
