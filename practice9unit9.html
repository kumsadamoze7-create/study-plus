1. What is statistical data?

Answer: Information collected for analysis.

Explanation: Statistical data are facts or observations collected to study a particular problem or situation.

2. What are the two main types of data?

Answer: Primary and secondary data.

Explanation: Primary data are collected directly by the researcher. Secondary data are obtained from existing sources.

3. What is primary data?

Answer: Data collected directly.

Explanation: Primary data are obtained firsthand from the original source by the researcher.

4. What is secondary data?

Answer: Data obtained from an existing source.

Explanation: Examples include books, reports, websites and previous research.

5. Which method involves asking people questions?

Answer: Interview.

Explanation: An interview collects information by directly asking questions to respondents.

6. What is direct observation?

Answer: Collecting data by observing directly.

Explanation: The researcher watches an event or behavior and records what happens.

7. Find the mean of 4, 6, 8, 10.

Answer: 7

Explanation: Mean = (4 + 6 + 8 + 10) ÷ 4
= 28 ÷ 4
= 7

8. Find the median of 3, 5, 7, 9, 11.

Answer: 7

Explanation: The numbers are already ordered. The middle value is 7, so the median is 7.

9. Find the mode of 2, 3, 3, 4, 5.

Answer: 3

Explanation: The mode is the value that occurs most frequently. The number 3 occurs twice.

10. Find the range of 5, 8, 12, 15.

Answer: 10

Explanation: Range = Maximum − Minimum
= 15 − 5
= 10

11. What is probability?

Answer: The chance of an event occurring.

Explanation: Probability measures how likely an event is to happen.

12. What is the probability of getting a head when a fair coin is tossed?

Answer: 1/2

Explanation: There are 2 equally likely outcomes: Head and Tail. Therefore: P(Head) = 1 ÷ 2 = 1/2

13. What is a sample space?

Answer: The set of all possible outcomes.

Explanation: For a coin toss, the sample space is {H,T}.

14. What is an event in probability?

Answer: A collection of outcomes of an experiment.

Explanation: An event can contain one or more possible outcomes.

15. What is the probability of an impossible event?

Answer: 0

Explanation: An impossible event cannot occur, therefore its probability is zero.

16. What is the probability of a certain event?

Answer: 1

Explanation: A certain event always occurs, so its probability is 1.

17. A die has how many possible outcomes?

Answer: 6

Explanation: A standard die has faces numbered 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6.

18. What is P(rolling a 6) on a fair die?

Answer: 1/6

Explanation: There is one favorable outcome (6) out of six possible outcomes. P(6) = 1/6

19. What is a histogram used to represent?

Answer: Frequency distribution.

Explanation: A histogram represents numerical data grouped into intervals.

20. What graph represents data using sectors of a circle?

Answer: Pie chart.

Explanation: A pie chart divides a circle into sectors representing different parts of a whole.

21. What is the sum of all probabilities in a sample space?

Answer: 1

Explanation: All possible outcomes together form the complete sample space, so their probabilities add to 1.

22. Find the mean of 10, 20, 30.

Answer: 20

Explanation: Mean = (10 + 20 + 30) ÷ 3 = 60 ÷ 3 = 20

23. Find the median of 2, 4, 6, 8.

Answer: 5

Explanation: There are two middle numbers: 4 and 6. Median = (4 + 6) ÷ 2 = 5

24. Find the range of 2, 7, 10, 15.

Answer: 13

Range = 15 − 2 = 13

25. What is experimental probability?

Answer: Probability based on actual experiment results.

Explanation: Experimental probability is calculated using observed outcomes. P(E) = frequency of E ÷ total trials.

26. A coin is tossed 20 times and heads appear 12 times. Find experimental P(H).

Answer: 0.6

P(H) = 12 ÷ 20 = 0.6

27. What is the maximum possible probability?

Answer: 1

Explanation: Probability always lies between 0 and 1 inclusive.

28. What is the minimum possible probability?

Answer: 0

Explanation: An impossible event has probability 0.

29. What measure of central tendency is most frequent?

Answer: Mode

Explanation: The mode is the value that occurs most often.

30. Which measure is the middle value of ordered data?

Answer: Median

Explanation: The median divides ordered data into two equal parts.

31. Which measure is calculated by adding all values and dividing by their number?

Answer: Mean

Mean = Sum of values ÷ Number of values.

32. What does standard deviation measure?

Answer: Dispersion or spread of data.

Explanation: Standard deviation tells how far data values tend to be from the mean.

33. What is variance related to?

Answer: Standard deviation.

Explanation: Variance is the square of the standard deviation.

34. If variance is 25, what is standard deviation?

Answer: 5

Standard deviation = √variance = √25 = 5

35. What is a frequency?

Answer: Number of times a value occurs.

36. What is a frequency distribution?

Answer: A table showing values and their frequencies.

37. What is the total angle of a complete circle?

Answer: 360°

A full circle contains 360 degrees.

38. In a pie chart, what does a sector represent?

Answer: A part of the whole.

39. What is the formula for probability of an event?

Answer: P(E) = Favorable outcomes ÷ Total possible outcomes.

40. What is a tree diagram used for?

Answer: Showing possible outcomes.

Explanation: A tree diagram organizes sequences of events and their possible outcomes.

41. Find P(even number) when rolling a fair die.

Answer: 1/2

Even outcomes = {2,4,6} → 3 outcomes. P = 3/6 = 1/2

42. Find P(odd number) on a fair die.

Answer: 1/2

Odd outcomes = {1,3,5}. P = 3/6 = 1/2

Answer: 7.5

Mean = (5+5+10+10) ÷ 4 = 30 ÷ 4 = 7.5

44. Find the mode of 1, 2, 2, 3, 4.

Answer: 2

2 occurs twice, more than any other value.

45. Find the median of 1, 3, 5, 7, 9.

Answer: 5

The middle value is 5.

46. Find the range of 20, 25, 30, 40.

Answer: 20

Range = 40 − 20 = 20

47. If P(A)=0.3, what is P(not A)?

Answer: 0.7

P(not A) = 1 − P(A) = 1 − 0.3 = 0.7

48. What is theoretical probability based on?

Answer: Equally likely possible outcomes.

49. What is data interpretation?

Answer: Finding meaning from collected data.

50. What is data analysis?

Answer: Examining data to find useful information.

51. A bag contains 3 red and 2 blue balls. Find P(red).

Answer: 3/5

Total balls = 3 + 2 = 5. Red balls = 3. P(red) = 3/5.

52. A bag contains 4 green and 6 yellow balls. Find P(green).

Answer: 2/5

P(green) = 4/10 = 2/5

53. What is the complement of an event?

Answer: The event that the original event does not occur.

54. What is the probability of getting a number greater than 4 on a die?

Answer: 1/3

Numbers greater than 4 are 5 and 6. P = 2/6 = 1/3

55. What is the purpose of organizing data?

Answer: To make data easier to understand and analyze.

56. Which chart is best for showing parts of a whole?

Answer: Pie chart.

57. Which graph uses connected bars for grouped numerical data?

Answer: Histogram.

58. If a fair coin is tossed twice, how many outcomes are possible?

Answer: 4

Sample space: HH, HT, TH, TT Therefore there are 4 outcomes.

59. What is the probability of getting two heads when a fair coin is tossed twice?

Answer: 1/4

Possible outcomes: HH, HT, TH, TT Only HH gives two heads. P = 1/4.

60. What is the probability of an event that always occurs?

Answer: 1

A certain event always occurs, so its probability is 1.
