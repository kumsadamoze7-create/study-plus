1. What is the mean of 2, 4, 6 and 8?

The sum is 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 = 20. There are 4 numbers. Mean = 20 ÷ 4 = 5.

Answer: B. 5

2. What is the median of 3, 7, 9, 11 and 15?

The numbers are already arranged in order. The middle number is 9.

Answer: C. 9

3. What is the mode of 2, 3, 3, 4, 5?

The mode is the number that occurs most frequently. 3 occurs twice.

Answer: B. 3

4. What is the range of 4, 7, 10 and 15?

Range = Maximum − Minimum. 15 − 4 = 11.

Answer: C. 11

5. Which one is a measure of central tendency?

Mean, median and mode are measures of central tendency.

Answer: A. Mean

6. Which graph is commonly used to represent continuous frequency data?

A histogram is used to represent continuous grouped frequency data.

Answer: A. Histogram

7. A pie chart represents data using what?

A pie chart divides a circle into sectors.

Answer: B. Sectors of a circle

8. What is the probability of getting a head when tossing a fair coin?

A fair coin has two equally likely outcomes: Head and Tail. Therefore P(Head) = 1/2.

Answer: C. 1/2

9. What is the probability of rolling a 6 on a fair die?

A die has 6 equally likely outcomes. Only one outcome is 6. Therefore P(6) = 1/6.

Answer: C. 1/6

10. What is the probability of an impossible event?

An impossible event can never happen, so its probability is 0.

Answer: A. 0

11. What is the probability of a certain event?

A certain event will definitely happen. Therefore its probability is 1.

Answer: C. 1

12. Which of the following is a primary data collection method?

Interviewing people directly is a method of collecting primary data.

Answer: A. Interview

13. Which one is secondary data?

Published reports are data collected previously by another source.

Answer: C. Published report

14. What is the square root of the variance called?

Standard deviation is the square root of variance.

Answer: C. Standard deviation

15. What does variance measure?

Variance measures how spread out the data values are around the mean.

Answer: B. Spread of data

16. If the data values are 5, 5, 5, 5, what is the range?

Range = maximum − minimum. 5 − 5 = 0.

Answer: A. 0

17. A sample space contains what?

The sample space is the set of all possible outcomes of an experiment.

Answer: A. All possible outcomes

18. What is an event in probability?

An event is a set or collection of one or more outcomes from the sample space.

Answer: A. A collection of outcomes

19. Experimental probability is based on what?

Experimental probability is calculated from the results of an actual experiment.

Answer: A. Actual experiments

20. What is the probability of getting an even number when rolling a fair die?

Even numbers on a die are 2, 4 and 6. There are 3 favorable outcomes out of 6. 3/6 = 1/2.

Answer: C. 1/2
