🧪 Chemistry

Grade 9 Chemistry — Unit 1 to Unit 5 are available.

🧪 Grade 9 Chemistry

Choose a unit to study Notes or Practice.

🧪 Unit 1 — Chemistry and Its Importance

Definition and scope of chemistry, matter, substance, properties, composition, branches of chemistry, relationship with other natural sciences, agriculture, food production, medicine, analgesics, antibiotics, disinfectants, construction materials and chemical industries.

📏 Unit 2 — Measurement and Scientific Method

Measurement, SI units, fundamental and derived quantities, measuring instruments, density, accuracy, precision, significant figures, scientific notation, scientific method, variables, laboratory safety and experimental error.

⚛️ Unit 3 — Structure of the Atom

Atomic theory, development of atomic models, subatomic particles, protons, neutrons and electrons, atomic number, mass number, isotopes, electron arrangement and ions.

🧩 Unit 4 — Periodic Classification of Elements

Historical development, Dobereiner triads, Newlands law of octaves, Mendeleev periodic law, periodicity, modern periodic table, groups and periods, s-, p-, d-, f-blocks, representative elements, ionization energy, electron affinity and electronegativity.

🔗 Unit 5 — Chemical Bonding

Chemical bonding, valence electrons, octet and duet rules, ionic and covalent bonding, single/double/triple covalent bonds, coordinate covalent bonding, metallic bonding, Lewis structures, electronegativity, polar and non-polar covalent bonds, ions and compounds.

🧬 Grade 9 Biology

Choose a unit to study Notes or Practice.

🔬 Unit 1 — Biology and Its History

Introduction to biology, characteristics of living things, branches of biology, scientific method, important scientists and their contributions, classification, taxonomy and development of biological knowledge.

🦠 Unit 2 — Classification of Living Organisms

Classification of organisms, taxonomy, taxonomic hierarchy, binomial nomenclature, kingdoms of life, characteristics of the major groups, and the Five Kingdom classification system.

Cell theory, prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, plant and animal cells, cell structures and organelles, functions of organelles, cell membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplasts, cell division and organization.

🌱 Unit 4 — Reproduction

Reproduction in living organisms, asexual and sexual reproduction, binary fission, budding, fragmentation, spore formation, vegetative propagation, reproductive organs, gametes, fertilization, zygote formation, human reproduction, pregnancy and reproductive health.
