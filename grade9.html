📘 Unit 1 — Further on Sets

Sets, elements, types of sets, subsets, cardinality, union, intersection, difference, complement, set laws, De Morgan's laws and Venn diagrams.

🔢 Unit 2 — The Number System

Natural numbers, integers, Euclidean division, prime and composite numbers, divisibility, GCF, LCM, rational numbers, irrational numbers, real numbers, exponents and radicals.

📕 Unit 3 — Solving Equations

Linear equations in one variable, systems of linear equations in two variables, substitution, elimination, graphical method, absolute value equations, quadratic equations, factorization, completing the square, quadratic formula, discriminant, exponents and radicals.

📙 Unit 4 — Solving Inequalities

Linear inequalities in one variable, systems of linear inequalities in two variables, graphical solutions, absolute value inequalities, quadratic inequalities, product properties and sign charts.

📐 Unit 5 — Introduction to Trigonometry

Revision of right-angled triangles, Pythagoras theorem, trigonometric ratios, basic trigonometric values, and trigonometric ratios from 0° to 90°.

🔷 Unit 6 — Regular Polygons

Convex and concave polygons, names of polygons, sum of interior angles, sum of exterior angles, interior and exterior angles of regular polygons, pentagon, hexagon, octagon, decagon, lines of symmetry, inscribed and circumscribed polygons, central angles, perimeter, area and apothem.

🔺 Unit 7 — Congruency and Similarity

Congruent and similar figures, definition of similar figures, similarity theorems, SSS similarity theorem, SAS similarity theorem, ratio of perimeters, ratio of areas, construction of similar figures and applications of similarity.

➡️ Unit 8 — Vectors in Two Dimensions

Vector and scalar quantities, representation of vectors, equality and opposite vectors, parallel vectors, column vectors, magnitude and direction, vector addition and subtraction, triangle law, parallelogram law, scalar multiplication, position vectors and applications of vectors.
