📚 Grade 9 Mathematics

Study Plus

Learn • Practice • Prepare for Entrance Exam

📘 Unit 1 — Further on Sets

Sets, subsets, union, intersection, complement, Venn diagrams and set operations.

🔢 Unit 2 — The Number System

Natural numbers, integers, Euclid division, prime and composite numbers, divisibility, GCF, LCM, rational numbers, irrational numbers, real numbers, exponents and radicals.

📕 Unit 3 — Solving Equations

Linear equations, systems of linear equations, quadratic equations, absolute value equations, equations involving exponents and radicals.

📗 Unit 4 — Solving Inequalities

Linear inequalities, systems of inequalities, graphical solutions, absolute value inequalities and quadratic inequalities.

📐 Unit 5 — Introduction to Trigonometry

Right-angled triangles, Pythagoras theorem, trigonometric ratios, basic angles and trigonometric ratios from 0° to 90°.

🔷 Unit 6 — Regular Polygons

Interior and exterior angles, convex and concave polygons, regular polygons, symmetry, pentagon, hexagon, octagon, decagon, inscribed polygons, perimeter, area and apothem.

🔺 Unit 7 — Congruency and Similarity

Congruent and similar figures, similarity theorems, SSS similarity, SAS similarity, ratios of perimeters, ratios of areas and applications of similarity.

➡️ Unit 8 — Vectors in Two Dimensions

Vector and scalar quantities, representation of vectors, equality, opposite and parallel vectors, column vectors, magnitude, direction, vector addition and subtraction, scalar multiplication, position vectors and applications.

📊 Unit 9 — Statistics and Probability

Statistical data, data collection, primary and secondary data, interviews, observation, organization and presentation of data, pie charts, frequency distributions, histograms, mean, median, mode, range, variance, standard deviation, experimental probability, sample space, events, tree diagrams and theoretical probability.
