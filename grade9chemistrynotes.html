🧪 Grade 9 Chemistry

Choose a unit to study

🧪 Unit 1 — Chemistry and Its Importance

Chemistry and its definition, scope and importance. Matter, substances, properties and composition. Branches of chemistry and the relationship between chemistry and other natural sciences.

📏 Unit 2 — Measurement and Scientific Method

Measurement, SI units, fundamental and derived quantities, measuring instruments, density, accuracy, precision, significant figures, scientific notation and scientific method.

⚛️ Unit 3 — Structure of the Atom

Atomic theory, development of atomic models, subatomic particles, protons, neutrons, electrons, atomic number, mass number, isotopes, electron arrangement and ions.

🧩 Unit 4 — Periodic Classification of Elements

Historical development of the periodic table, Dobereiner triads, Newlands law of octaves, Mendeleev periodic law, modern periodic table, groups, periods, blocks and periodic properties.

🔗 Unit 5 — Chemical Bonding

Chemical bonding, valence electrons, octet and duet rules, ionic bonding, covalent bonding, coordinate covalent bonding, metallic bonding, Lewis structures and electronegativity.
