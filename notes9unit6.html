6.1 🔷 Sum of Interior Angles of a Convex Polygon

What is a Polygon?

A polygon is a closed plane figure made from straight line segments.

Triangle → 3 sides
Quadrilateral → 4 sides
Pentagon → 5 sides
Hexagon → 6 sides

Convex Polygon

A polygon is convex if all its interior angles are less than 180°. Also, all diagonals lie inside the polygon.

Concave Polygon

A polygon is concave if at least one interior angle is greater than 180°.

⭐ Convex → no angle greater than 180°

⭐ Concave → at least one angle greater than 180°

Deriving the Formula

An n-sided convex polygon can be divided into triangles by drawing diagonals from one vertex.

The number of triangles formed is:

Number of triangles = n − 2

Since the sum of the angles in each triangle is 180°:

Sum of interior angles = (n − 2) × 180°
Example: Find the sum of the interior angles of a hexagon.

n = 6

(6 − 2) × 180°
= 4 × 180°
= 720°
⚡ Entrance Trick:

Remember: (n − 2) × 180°

Just subtract 2 from the number of sides and multiply by 180°.

6.2 🔶 Sum of Exterior Angles

Exterior Angle

An exterior angle is formed when one side of a polygon is extended.

Sum of exterior angles of any convex polygon = 360°
Example: A pentagon has exterior angles.

Sum = 360°

Why is the sum 360°?

If you travel around a polygon, you make one complete turn. One complete turn is 360°.

⚡ Very Important:

Interior angle sum depends on n.

Exterior angle sum is ALWAYS: 360°

Sum of Exterior Angles of an n-sided Polygon

Sum of exterior angles = 360°

This is true for every convex polygon, regardless of the number of sides.

6.3 📏 Measures of Interior and Exterior Angles

Regular Polygon

A regular polygon has all sides equal and all interior angles equal.

Each Interior Angle

First find the total interior angle sum:

(n − 2) × 180°

Then divide by n:

Each interior angle = ((n − 2) × 180°) / n
Example: Find each interior angle of a regular pentagon.

n = 5

[(5 − 2) × 180°] ÷ 5
= 540° ÷ 5
= 108°

Each Exterior Angle

Each exterior angle = 360° / n
Example: Find each exterior angle of a regular hexagon.

n = 6

360° ÷ 6
= 60°

Relationship Between Interior and Exterior Angle

Interior angle + Exterior angle = 180°
If exterior angle = 40°:

Interior angle = 180° − 40°
= 140°
⚡ Fast Method:

Exterior = 360 ÷ n

Interior = 180 − Exterior

6.4 ⭐ Properties of Regular Polygons

A regular polygon has:

  • Equal sides
  • Equal interior angles
  • Equal exterior angles
  • Equal central angles
  • Lines of symmetry

Regular Pentagon

Sides = 5
Each exterior angle = 360 ÷ 5 = 72°
Each interior angle = 180 − 72 = 108°
Lines of symmetry = 5

Regular Hexagon

Sides = 6
Each exterior angle = 360 ÷ 6 = 60°
Each interior angle = 180 − 60 = 120°
Lines of symmetry = 6

Regular Octagon

Sides = 8
Each exterior angle = 360 ÷ 8 = 45°
Each interior angle = 180 − 45 = 135°
Lines of symmetry = 8

Regular Decagon

Sides = 10
Each exterior angle = 360 ÷ 10 = 36°
Each interior angle = 180 − 36 = 144°
Lines of symmetry = 10

⭕ Central Angle of a Regular Polygon

The central angle is the angle formed at the center of a circle by two consecutive vertices of a regular polygon.

Central angle = 360° / n
Find the central angle of a regular octagon.

n = 8

360° ÷ 8
= 45°
⭐ For a regular polygon:

Central angle = Exterior angle

⭕ Inscribed and Circumscribed Polygons

Inscribed Polygon

A polygon is inscribed in a circle when all its vertices lie on the circle.

Circumscribed Polygon

A polygon is circumscribed about a circle when each side of the polygon is tangent to the circle.

Remember:

Inscribed → vertices touch the circle.

Circumscribed → sides touch the circle.
⚡ Memory Trick:

INSIDE → INSCRIBED
CIRCLE INSIDE → CIRCUMSCRIBED

📐 Apothem of a Regular Polygon

The apothem is the perpendicular distance from the center of a regular polygon to one of its sides.

The apothem meets the side at a right angle (90°).

Area Using Apothem

Area = ½ × perimeter × apothem
A regular polygon has:

Perimeter = 40 cm
Apothem = 5 cm

Area = ½ × 40 × 5
= 100 cm²

Perimeter

Perimeter = n × side length
A regular hexagon has side length 8 cm.

P = 6 × 8
= 48 cm

⭕ Regular Polygon Inscribed in a Circle

When a regular polygon is inscribed in a circle, all of its vertices lie on the circle.

Central Angle

θ = 360° / n

Area

A = ½Pa

where:

  • A = area
  • P = perimeter
  • a = apothem

Perimeter

P = ns

where s is the side length.

Example: A regular polygon has 8 sides and each side is 6 cm.

P = ns
= 8 × 6
= 48 cm

⚠️ Common Mistakes

❌ Mistake 1: Confusing interior angle sum with each interior angle.

❌ Mistake 2: Using 180° instead of 360° for exterior angle sum.

❌ Mistake 3: Forgetting to divide the interior angle sum by n when finding each interior angle.

❌ Mistake 4: Confusing apothem with side length.

❌ Mistake 5: Forgetting that regular polygons have equal sides and equal angles.
