📘 Unit 6 Overview

A polygon is a closed plane figure made from straight line segments.

In this unit we study polygons, regular polygons, interior angles, exterior angles, perimeter, and relationships between angles and sides.

Regular polygon:
A polygon in which all sides are equal in length and all interior angles are equal.
⚡ Easy Memory:

Regular polygon = Equal sides + Equal angles.

6.1 What is a Polygon?

The word polygon comes from words meaning "many angles."

A polygon is a closed two-dimensional figure formed only by straight line segments.

6.2 Regular and Irregular Polygons

Regular Polygon

All sides are equal and all interior angles are equal.
Example: A square is a regular polygon because:
  • All four sides are equal.
  • All four interior angles are 90°.

Irregular Polygon

A polygon that does not have all sides and angles equal.
⚡ Remember:
Regular → everything is equal.
Irregular → not everything is equal.

6.3 Sum of Interior Angles

The sum of the interior angles of an n-sided polygon is:

(n − 2) × 180°

Example: Pentagon

A pentagon has 5 sides.
(5 − 2) × 180°
3 × 180° = 540°
Therefore, the sum of its interior angles is: 540°

Example: Hexagon

(6 − 2) × 180° = 720°
⚡ Fast Trick:

Number of sides − 2
then × 180°.

6.4 Interior Angle of a Regular Polygon

For a regular polygon, all interior angles are equal. Therefore, divide the total interior angle sum by the number of sides.

Interior angle = [(n − 2) × 180°] / n

Example: Regular Hexagon

n = 6
[(6 − 2) × 180°] / 6
720° / 6 = 120°
Therefore: Each interior angle = 120°
⚡ Trick:
First find the total angle sum, then divide by the number of sides.

6.5 Exterior Angles

An exterior angle is formed when one side of a polygon is extended.

Sum of exterior angles = 360°

This is true for every polygon.

Regular Polygon Exterior Angle

Exterior angle = 360° / n

Example: Regular Pentagon

n = 5
360° / 5 = 72°
Therefore: Each exterior angle = 72°
⚡ Golden Trick:
For a regular polygon: Exterior angle = 360 ÷ number of sides.

6.6 Relationship Between Interior and Exterior Angles

At each vertex, the interior angle and its corresponding exterior angle form a straight line.

Interior angle + Exterior angle = 180°
For a regular hexagon: Interior angle = 120° Exterior angle = 60°
120° + 60° = 180°
⚡ Remember:

Interior + Exterior = 180°
Exterior angles together = 360°

6.7 Perimeter of a Regular Polygon

Perimeter is the total distance around a polygon.

Perimeter = number of sides × length of one side

P = ns
A regular pentagon has side length 8 cm.
P = 5 × 8
P = 40 cm
⚡ Trick:
Regular polygon?
Multiply side × number of sides.

6.8 Finding the Number of Sides

If the exterior angle of a regular polygon is known, we can find the number of sides.

n = 360° / exterior angle
The exterior angle of a regular polygon is 45°. Find the number of sides.
n = 360° / 45°
n = 8
Therefore, it is a regular octagon.

6.9 Finding the Number of Sides from an Interior Angle

For a regular polygon:

Interior angle = 180° − exterior angle
The interior angle is 150°. First find the exterior angle:
180° − 150° = 30°
Then:
n = 360° / 30°
n = 12
Therefore, the polygon has 12 sides.
⚡ Two-Step Trick:

Interior → subtract from 180°
Then 360° ÷ exterior angle.

6.10 Diagonals of a Polygon

A diagonal is a line segment joining two non-adjacent vertices of a polygon.

Number of diagonals = n(n − 3) / 2

Example: Pentagon

n = 5
5(5 − 3) / 2
5 × 2 / 2 = 5
A pentagon has 5 diagonals.
⚡ Diagonal Trick:
n × (n − 3), then divide by 2.

6.11 Triangulation of a Polygon

A polygon can be divided into triangles by drawing diagonals from one vertex.

An n-sided polygon can be divided into:

n − 2 triangles
A hexagon:
6 − 2 = 4 triangles
Therefore, a hexagon can be divided into 4 triangles.
⚡ Trick:
Sides − 2 = number of triangles.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Do not confuse the total interior angle sum with one interior angle.
  • Do not use 360° as the interior angle sum.
  • Remember that the exterior angles of a polygon add to 360°.
  • For a regular polygon, divide the interior angle sum by n.
  • When finding perimeter, count all sides.
  • Do not confuse number of sides with number of diagonals.

⚡ Quick Revision

  • A polygon is a closed figure made from straight sides.
  • A regular polygon has equal sides and equal angles.
  • Interior angle sum = (n − 2) × 180°.
  • Regular interior angle = [(n − 2) × 180°] ÷ n.
  • Exterior angle of a regular polygon = 360° ÷ n.
  • Interior angle + exterior angle = 180°.
  • Exterior angles always total 360°.
  • Perimeter = n × side length.
  • Number of diagonals = n(n − 3) ÷ 2.
  • Number of triangles from one vertex = n − 2.

🎯 Unit 6 Exam Tips

  • If they ask for total interior angles, use (n − 2) × 180°.
  • If they ask for one regular interior angle, divide by n.
  • If they give an exterior angle, use 360° ÷ exterior angle to find n.
  • If they give an interior angle, first calculate 180° − interior angle.
  • For perimeter, multiply the number of sides by one side length.
  • For diagonals, remember n(n − 3) ÷ 2.
  • Write the formula before substituting numbers in an exam.
