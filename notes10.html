📘 Unit 1: Algebra

What is Algebra?

Algebra is a branch of mathematics that uses letters and symbols to represent unknown numbers.

Example

If x + 5 = 12

x = 12 - 5

x = 7

Remember ⭐

When solving an equation, whatever operation you perform on one side should also be performed on the other side.
