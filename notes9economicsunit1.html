📖 Unit Introduction

Economics is an important social science that helps us understand how people, households, businesses and governments make choices when resources are limited but human wants are many.

Economics is useful in everyday life because people constantly make decisions about earning, spending, saving, producing, buying and using resources.

Main idea:
Economics studies how scarce resources can be allocated efficiently to satisfy human needs and wants as much as possible.

Why do we study Economics?

  • To understand how resources are allocated.
  • To understand how people make economic choices.
  • To understand production, consumption and distribution.
  • To understand economic problems such as poverty and unemployment.
  • To improve logical and analytical thinking.
  • To understand the behavior of households, firms and government.
  • To make better economic decisions in everyday life.

1.1 Meaning and Nature of Economics

Meaning of Economics

The word economics comes from the ancient Greek word oikonomia, which refers to the management of a household or family.

Today economics is a branch of the social sciences. It studies how people and society make choices concerning the use of limited resources to satisfy human needs and wants.

Definition:
Economics is a social science concerned with the efficient allocation of scarce resources to satisfy human needs and wants.

Important ideas contained in the definition

  1. Human needs and wants are many: People continuously want food, clothing, housing, education, transport, entertainment and many other goods and services.
  2. Resources are scarce: The resources used to produce goods and services are limited relative to human wants.
  3. Choice is necessary: Because resources are limited, people must choose among different alternatives.
  4. Allocation is necessary: Available resources must be distributed among competing uses.
  5. Efficiency is important: Resources should be used carefully so that waste is minimized and satisfaction is increased.
