Study Plus

📘 Unit 1

Introduction to Physics and basic concepts of physical science.

📏 Unit 2 — Physical Quantities and Measurement

Physical quantities, fundamental and derived quantities, SI units, measurement, measuring instruments, accuracy, precision, errors and scientific notation.

🏃 Unit 3 — Motion

Motion, reference point, distance, displacement, speed, velocity, acceleration and motion graphs.

💪 Unit 4 — Force and Newton's Laws

Force, types of forces, Newton's laws of motion, mass, weight, friction and applications of force.

⚙️ Unit 5 — Simple Machines

Simple machines, types of simple machines, mechanical advantage, velocity ratio and efficiency. Levers, pulleys, inclined planes, wheel and axle, screw and wedge.

〰️ Unit 6 — Mechanical Oscillation and Sound Wave

Mechanical oscillation, periodic motion, amplitude, period, frequency, simple harmonic motion, sound production, sound waves, wavelength, frequency, wave speed and applications of sound.

🌡️ Unit 7 — Temperature and Thermometer

Temperature, thermal equilibrium, temperature scales, thermometers, Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin scales, calibration and measurement of temperature.
