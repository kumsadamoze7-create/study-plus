🧬 Grade 9 Biology

📖 Notes

Study Plus

🧬 Unit 1 — Biology and Its Applications

Introduction to Biology, characteristics of living organisms, branches of Biology and applications of Biology in everyday life.

🔬 Unit 2 — Classification of Organisms

Classification of living organisms, taxonomy, five kingdoms, characteristics of the major kingdoms and scientific naming.

🧫 Unit 3 — Cell Biology

Cell structure, cell organelles, functions of organelles, plant and animal cells and differences between them.

🌱 Unit 4 — Plant Biology

Plant structure, tissues, roots, stems, leaves, photosynthesis and plant functions.

🐾 Unit 5 — Animal Biology

Animal groups, major characteristics, classification of animals and basic animal structures.

🧬 Unit 6 — Human Biology

Basic human body systems, nutrition, respiration, circulation and body functions.

🌍 Unit 7 — Ecology

Ecosystems, habitats, populations, communities, food chains, food webs and environmental relationships.

🌿 Unit 8 — Biodiversity and Conservation

Biodiversity, threats to biodiversity, conservation methods and sustainable use of resources.

🌎 Unit 9 — Environment and Natural Resources

Natural resources, environmental problems, pollution, resource management and environmental protection.
