Study Plus

🧬 Unit 1 — Introduction to Biology

Study the basic concepts of Biology, its importance, branches, scientific methods and applications of Biology.

🔬 Unit 2 — Characteristics and Classification of Organisms

Characteristics of living organisms, classification, taxonomy, scientific naming and major groups of organisms.

🧫 Unit 3 — Cells

Cell theory, cell structure, cell organelles, plant and animal cells and organization of living things.

🧬 Unit 4 — Reproduction

Asexual and sexual reproduction, human reproductive system, fertilization, pregnancy and reproductive health.

🥗 Unit 5 — Human Health, Nutrition, and Disease

Nutrition, nutrients, balanced diet, malnutrition, diseases, prevention and human health.

🌍 Unit 6 — Ecology

Ecology, ecosystems, biotic and abiotic factors, ecological relationships, succession and biomes.
