🧬 Grade 9 Biology

📖 Notes

Study Plus

🧬 Unit 1 — Introduction to Biology

Biology as a science, the importance of Biology, scientific methods, tools used by biologists, microscope and laboratory safety.

🔬 Unit 2 — Living Things and Their Classification

Characteristics of living organisms, classification, taxonomy, Linnaean system, scientific naming and the five-kingdom system.

🧫 Unit 3 — Cells

Cell theory, cell structure, cell organelles, plant and animal cells, transport across the cell membrane and levels of biological organization.

🧬 Unit 4 — Reproduction

Reproduction, asexual and sexual reproduction, human reproductive systems, menstrual cycle, fertilization, pregnancy, birth control and STIs.

🥗 Unit 5 — Human Health, Nutrition, and Disease

Food and nutrition, nutrients, balanced diet, malnutrition, deficiency diseases, substance abuse, infectious and non-infectious diseases.

🌍 Unit 6 — Ecology

Ecological terms, biotic and abiotic components, ecological levels, ecosystems, terrestrial and aquatic biomes, ecological succession and ecological relationships.
