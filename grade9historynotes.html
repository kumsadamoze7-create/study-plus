Ancient civilizations, their political, social, economic and cultural developments up to about 500 AD.

Peoples, states, societies and historical developments in Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa up to the end of the 13th century.

Major political, social, economic and cultural developments during the Middle Ages and the early modern period.

States and principalities, population movements, political developments and interactions in Ethiopia from the 13th to the mid-16th century.

Political, social and economic developments in Ethiopia from the mid-16th to the mid-19th century.

🇫🇷 Unit 9 — The Age of Revolutions, 1750s–1815

The major revolutions and political changes from the 1750s to 1815, including their causes, events and consequences.
