🔵 Part C — Explain

56. Explain what periodicity means.

Expected idea: Periodicity is the regular repetition of similar physical and chemical properties when elements are arranged according to increasing atomic number.

57. Explain the difference between Mendeleev's periodic law and the modern periodic law.

Expected idea: Mendeleev's periodic law was based mainly on atomic mass, while the modern periodic law is based on atomic number.

58. Explain why ionization energy generally increases across a period.

Expected idea: Nuclear charge increases across the period while electrons are added to the same main energy level, so the nucleus attracts the outer electrons more strongly and more energy is needed to remove one.

59. Explain why atomic radius generally increases down a group.

Expected idea: Additional electron shells are added as we move down a group, increasing the distance between the nucleus and outer electrons.

60. Explain the difference between electron affinity and electronegativity.

Expected idea: Electron affinity describes the energy change when an isolated gaseous atom gains an electron, while electronegativity describes how strongly an atom in a chemical bond attracts shared electrons.
