New Curriculum Notes

Unit 1: Introducing Economics

This unit introduces the meaning and nature of economics, the branches of economics, methods and approaches of studying economics, and the major decision-making units in an economy.

Unit 2: The Basic Economic Problems and Economic Systems

This unit explains scarcity, choice and opportunity cost, the central economic problems, and different economic systems.

Unit 3: Economic Resources and Markets

This unit discusses economic resources, factor payments, renewable and non-renewable resources, markets, circular flow of economic activities, and land as an economic resource in Ethiopia.

Unit 4: Introduction to Demand and Supply

This unit introduces demand, supply, factors affecting demand and supply, and market equilibrium.

Unit 5: Introduction to Production and Cost

This unit explains production, inputs and outputs, periods of production, and the different components of production cost.

Unit 6: Introduction to Money

This unit explains the meaning and evolution of money, functions of money, demand and supply of money, and electronic money.

Unit 7: Introduction to Macroeconomics

This unit introduces macroeconomic variables, economic growth, unemployment, inflation, balance of payments, and major macroeconomic goals and problems.

Unit 8: Basic Entrepreneurship

This unit introduces enterprise, entrepreneur and entrepreneurship, creativity, innovation, entrepreneurial attitudes, opportunities, success, teamwork, finance and promotion of entrepreneurship.
