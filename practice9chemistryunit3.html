Unit 3 — Structure of the Atom

📝 Practice — 60 Questions

🎉 Practice Completed!

1. Which particle has a negative electrical charge?
Explanation: An electron has a negative charge. A proton has a positive charge, while a neutron has no electrical charge.
2. Which particle has a positive electrical charge?
Explanation: A proton carries a positive electrical charge.
3. Which subatomic particle has no electrical charge?
Explanation: A neutron is electrically neutral because it has no net charge.
4. Where are protons and neutrons mainly found?
Explanation: The nucleus contains protons and neutrons. Electrons occupy regions around the nucleus.
5. Who proposed the first modern atomic theory?
Explanation: John Dalton developed an early modern atomic theory in the early 19th century.
6. Who discovered the electron?
Explanation: J.J. Thomson identified the electron through cathode-ray experiments.
7. Who proposed the nuclear model of the atom?
Explanation: Ernest Rutherford's gold-foil experiment led to the nuclear model of the atom.
8. Who discovered the neutron?
Explanation: James Chadwick discovered the neutron in 1932.
9. What is the atomic number of an element?
Explanation: The atomic number is equal to the number of protons in the nucleus.
10. What is the mass number of an atom?
Explanation: Mass number is the total number of protons and neutrons in the nucleus.
11. An atom has 11 protons. What is its atomic number?
Explanation: Atomic number = number of protons. Therefore, the atomic number is 11.
12. An atom has 8 protons and 8 neutrons. What is its mass number?
Explanation: Mass number = protons + neutrons = 8 + 8 = 16.
13. A neutral atom has 17 protons. How many electrons does it have?
Explanation: In a neutral atom, the number of electrons equals the number of protons.
14. What is an isotope?
Explanation: Isotopes have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons.
15. Isotopes of an element have the same:
Explanation: Isotopes have the same number of protons, so they have the same atomic number.
16. An atom has atomic number 12 and mass number 24. How many neutrons does it have?
Explanation: Neutrons = Mass number − Atomic number 24 − 12 = 12 neutrons.
17. Which particle has the smallest mass among the three main subatomic particles?
Explanation: The electron has a much smaller mass than a proton or neutron.
18. What is the nucleus mainly made of?
Explanation: The nucleus contains protons and neutrons.
19. Which particle determines the identity of an element?
Explanation: The number of protons determines the atomic number and therefore the identity of an element.
20. What happens when a neutral atom loses electrons?
Explanation: Electrons have negative charge. Losing negative electrons leaves the atom with a net positive charge.
21. What happens when an atom gains electrons?
Explanation: Gaining negatively charged electrons gives the atom an overall negative charge.
22. A positively charged ion is called a:
Explanation: A cation is a positively charged ion.
23. A negatively charged ion is called a:
Explanation: An anion is a negatively charged ion.
24. Which atomic model is associated with a "plum pudding" model?
Explanation: J.J. Thomson proposed the plum-pudding model.
25. Rutherford's experiment showed that the atom contains:
Explanation: Rutherford's gold-foil experiment showed that most of the atom is empty space and that positive charge is concentrated in a small nucleus.
26. Most of the atom is:
Explanation: Rutherford's experiment demonstrated that most of an atom is empty space.
27. What is the charge of the nucleus?
Explanation: The nucleus contains positively charged protons and neutral neutrons, so the nucleus has an overall positive charge.
28. The number of protons in an atom is 15. What is its atomic number?
Explanation: Atomic number equals the number of protons.
29. An atom contains 13 protons and 14 neutrons. What is its mass number?
Explanation: Mass number = protons + neutrons 13 + 14 = 27.
30. An atom has mass number 35 and atomic number 17. How many neutrons are present?
Explanation: Neutrons = 35 − 17 = 18.
31. Which particle is found outside the nucleus?
Explanation: Electrons occupy regions around the nucleus.
32. Protons and neutrons together are called:
Explanation: Protons and neutrons are collectively called nucleons because they are located in the nucleus.
33. Which statement about isotopes is correct?
Explanation: Isotopes are atoms of the same element, so they have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons.
34. Which subatomic particle determines the chemical behavior of an atom most directly?
Explanation: The arrangement of electrons, especially outer electrons, strongly influences chemical behavior and bonding.
35. What is an atom?
Explanation: An atom is the basic unit of an element that retains the chemical identity of that element.
36. Which statement about electrons is correct?
Explanation: Electrons have a negative charge and occupy regions around the nucleus.
37. Which statement about neutrons is correct?
Explanation: Neutrons are electrically neutral particles found in the nucleus.
38. If a neutral atom has 20 electrons, how many protons does it have?
Explanation: A neutral atom has equal numbers of protons and electrons.
39. Which of the following changes the mass number of an atom?
Explanation: Mass number is the sum of protons and neutrons, so changing neutrons changes the mass number.
40. Which change produces an ion without changing the element's identity?
Explanation: Changing electrons changes the charge but not the atomic number, so the element remains the same.
41. An atom has 10 protons and 12 neutrons. What is its mass number?
Explanation: 10 + 12 = 22.
42. An atom has 25 protons and mass number 55. How many neutrons does it have?
Explanation: Neutrons = 55 − 25 = 30.
43. Which scientist's experiment used alpha particles and thin gold foil?
Explanation: Rutherford and his colleagues used the gold-foil experiment to investigate the structure of the atom.
44. Dalton's model considered atoms of the same element to be:
Explanation: Dalton's original theory treated atoms of the same element as fundamentally alike.
45. Which particle is directly involved in forming most chemical bonds?
Explanation: Chemical bonding mainly involves electrons, especially valence electrons.
46. What is the charge of a proton?
Explanation: A proton has a relative charge of +1.
47. What is the relative charge of an electron?
Explanation: An electron has a relative charge of −1.
48. What is the relative charge of a neutron?
Explanation: A neutron has zero electrical charge.
49. Which two particles have approximately equal masses?
Explanation: Protons and neutrons have approximately equal masses, while an electron is much lighter.
50. Which part of the atom contains most of its mass?
Explanation: Almost all of an atom's mass is concentrated in the nucleus, where protons and neutrons are located.
51. An atom has 16 protons and 18 electrons. What is its charge?
Explanation: Charge = protons − electrons 16 − 18 = −2. Therefore, the ion has a charge of −2.
52. An ion has 12 protons and 10 electrons. What is its charge?
Explanation: 12 protons − 10 electrons = +2. So the ion has a +2 charge.
53. If an atom loses two electrons, its charge becomes:
Explanation: Losing two negatively charged electrons leaves a net charge of +2.
54. If an atom gains three electrons, its charge becomes:
Explanation: Each electron has a −1 charge, so gaining three gives a −3 charge.
55. Which statement best describes an isotope?
Explanation: The defining feature of isotopes is the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons.
56. What determines the atomic number?
Explanation: Atomic number is defined as the number of protons in the nucleus.
57. What determines the mass number?
Explanation: Mass number is the total number of protons and neutrons.
58. Which model first introduced the idea of a small dense nucleus?
Explanation: Rutherford's nuclear model introduced the idea of a small, dense, positively charged nucleus.
59. Which statement is TRUE about a neutral atom?
Explanation: A neutral atom has equal numbers of positive protons and negative electrons, giving an overall charge of zero.
60. Which sequence correctly shows the main development of atomic models?
Explanation: Atomic theory developed progressively. Dalton proposed an early atomic theory, Thomson discovered the electron, and Rutherford developed the nuclear model. Later work further developed the modern atomic model.
