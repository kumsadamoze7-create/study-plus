📘 Unit 1: Calculus

What is a Derivative?

A derivative measures how quickly a function changes with respect to its variable.

Example

f(x) = x²

f′(x) = 2x

⭐ Remember

The derivative of xⁿ is n xⁿ⁻¹.

📘 Example 2

Find the derivative of f(x) = 3x² + 2x.

f′(x) = 6x + 2

Answer: 6x + 2
