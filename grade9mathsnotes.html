📐 Grade 9 Mathematics

📖 Notes

Study Plus

📘 Unit 1 — Further on Sets

Sets, subsets, universal sets, operations on sets, Venn diagrams, union, intersection, complement and applications of sets.

🔢 Unit 2 — The Number System

Natural numbers, integers, rational numbers, irrational numbers, real numbers and operations on the number system.

📕 Unit 3 — Solving Equations

Linear equations, equations with brackets, equations involving fractions and applications of equations.

📗 Unit 4 — Solving Inequalities

Linear inequalities, solving inequalities, number-line representation and applications of inequalities.

📐 Unit 5 — Introduction to Trigonometry

Angles, right triangles, sine, cosine, tangent and basic trigonometric relationships.

🔷 Unit 6 — Regular Polygons

Polygons, regular polygons, interior angles, exterior angles, perimeter and properties of regular polygons.

🔺 Unit 7 — Congruency and Similarity

Congruent figures, similar figures, triangles, congruency conditions and similarity relationships.

➡️ Unit 8 — Vectors in Two Dimensions

Vectors, magnitude, direction, vector addition, subtraction and representation of vectors in two dimensions.

📊 Unit 9 — Statistics and Probability

Data collection, tables, graphs, mean, median, mode, range, probability and interpretation of statistical information.
